# Code of Conduct — SET08103 Group 4

## Communication
- Primary channel: Microsoft Teams group chat
- Respond within 24 hours where possible; flag early if you'll be unavailable (illness, deadlines elsewhere)
- Be clear and specific when asking for help or reporting blockers

## Working Practices
- Follow the GitFlow branching model:
  - No direct commits to `master`
  - All feature work branches off `develop`
  - `release` branches are used to prepare and stabilise a version before merging to `master`
- Use Pull Requests to merge into `develop` or `master`, with at least one team member reviewing before merge
- Write clear, descriptive commit messages

## Meetings
- Sync during class breaks or scheduled Teams calls where possible
- Come prepared with a short update: what you've done, what you're doing next, and any blockers

## Respect and Collaboration
- Be respectful and constructive when giving feedback
- Raise concerns or disagreements early rather than letting them build up
- If you're stuck, ask for help rather than going silent

## Accountability
- Do your best to deliver on tasks you've claimed by the agreed time
- If circumstances change (illness, workload, personal matters), communicate as early as possible so tasks can be redistributed fairly
